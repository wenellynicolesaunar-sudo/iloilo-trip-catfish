Every restaurant on the itinerary — what to order and the insider tip.
Netong's Batchoy
Mega Bowl + ask for extra caldo & chicharon (libre!). Inside La Paz Market — the OG wet market experience.
Popoy's Batchoy
Sweeter, richer broth. Central Market. The local-local favorite — compare with Netong's!
Ted's Old Timer
Air-conditioned, consistent. Best for tired arrival days. Multiple branches near Megaworld.
Madge Cafe
Since 1940. Kapeng Barako + calamansi donuts. La Paz Market (original) or Atria branch near Megaworld.
Roberto's Siopao
Calle Real. Queen Siopao (bacon, sausage, chicken, adobo, salted egg) — select days only. If unavailable: The King.
Pancit Molo
Kap Isko's or Molo Mansion beside Molo Church. Wonton soup, shredded chicken, shrimp broth. Try fried molo balls!
Maridel's Cafe 🎂
Plazuela de Iloilo, near Megaworld. Mango Brazos, Frozen Lemon Meringue, Death by Chocolate, Snickers Pie. Eat ice cream cakes fast — they melt!
Breakthrough
Villa Beach. Baked Talaba, Aligue Rice, Managat (grilled or sinigang). Book ahead for groups!
Tatoy's Manokan
Villa Beach. Native Lechon Manok (lemongrass, charcoal-roasted), Kamonsil (baked scallops). Book ahead!
Pit Stop, Guimaras
Mango pizza — a must! Fresh mangoes, mango jam & dried mango for pasalubong.
Patpat's Kansi House
Jaro. Sour beef marrow soup with batwan fruit. Extra bone marrow. Stop on the Miag-ao drive back.
Ponsyon by Breakthrough
City branch, easier to book. Binakol, sizzling kansi, crispy pata. Ponsyon = Ilonggo for "feast."
Ang Kamalig
Home of Ilonggo cuisine. KBL, binakol, crispy pata, fresh lumpia. Cozy, family-style, great value.
Y2K Talabahan
Smallville strip. Fresh oysters, grilled seafood. Casual, open-air, local crowd. Steps from Megaworld.
💡 Pro tips: Netong's + Madge Cafe are side by side in La Paz Market — do both in one morning. Breakthrough and Tatoy's are next to each other at Villa Beach — pick one each night to try both. Maridel's is at Plazuela de Iloilo, right beside Ponsyon — grab dessert there after dinner too!
